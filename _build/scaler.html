Any recipe.
Any crowd.

Paste any ingredient list and say who's eating. Kids count as half a portion. You get amounts you can actually measure, like "1/4 cup + 2 tbsp" instead of 0.375 cups, eggs rounded up, butter in sticks, and a heads-up when it's time to cook in batches.

Paste the ingredients

One ingredient per line, any format: 1 1/2 cups, 1½ cups, 3 Tbsp, 200 g, 2 large eggs.

A kid counts as half a portion. Set grown-ups to the whole group if everyone eats like a grown-up.
