## Map

### Voice checker (voicecheck.html)
- Promise (title, search description, intro): "Paste words you wrote with AI. I'll mark the sentences that give it away and tell you what to put there instead."
- Inputs: one textarea, "Your words". No limit on the box; the check stops at about 3,000 words with a message.
- Buttons: Check it, Try a sample, and a "Want me to fix it?" link under the result.
- Output: a heading "N of M sentences have marks" (or "Nothing jumped out…"), one card per marked sentence in text order, each with the rule and a suggestion. Text that doesn't read as English gets "This reads English only, so I can't check these words."
- State: none kept. No hash, no localStorage.

### Busywork receipt (receipt.html)
- Promise: "Tap the chores that eat your week. See what a year of them costs you." Search description adds "and which ones could honestly come back."
- Inputs: hourly rate (number, min 0, step 1, default 30). 12 chore chips plus "+ Something else". Each line: name, minutes (number, min 0, step 5), per (a workday ×250, a week ×50, a month ×12), and for "Something else" a "What fixes it?" select. Change and × on each line.
- Starts with 3 lines: Answering the same questions 30/day, Copying info between apps 30/day, Writing social posts 90/week.
- Output: a printed receipt: one money and hours figure per line, TOTAL, Hours, Full work weeks, "Done all at once, starting New Year's Day, you'd finish on <date>", "You could get back", and "a Build from $3,000 would pay for itself in about N weeks".
- Buttons: Copy it (clipboard text), Print it, and a link to start.html carrying `#pile=…&never=…`.
- State: none kept.

### Reel shot list (shotlist.html)
- Promise: "Tell me what you run. You get 10 clips to film, in the order you walk your shop." Search description: "in edit order and in one-lap shooting order."
- Chips: type (Cafe or bakery, Restaurant or food truck, Shop or boutique, Salon barber or studio, Trades or repair, Gym or class, Nonprofit program), goal (Bring people in, Show how it's made, Introduce the people, Ask for support), length (15, 30, 45 s), light (Morning, Midday, Evening). Nonprofit sets the goal to Ask for support.
- Output: 10 clips in shooting order, the edit with seconds per clip, on-screen captions, a light tip, and a countdown to Small Business Saturday or Giving Tuesday that shows 0 to 42 days out.
- Buttons: Change the details, How it gets cut together, Copy the shot list, Print it.
- State: none kept.

### Caption fitter (captions.html)
- Promise (search description): "balanced line breaks, no stranded words, clear of each app's buttons, enough time to read, and a see-through PNG to drop on your video."
- Inputs: caption textarea; clip length (number, min 1, max 60, step 0.5, default 7.5, clamped on change); chips for size (Small, Medium, Large), where (Top, Middle, Bottom), box (Dark, Light, No box) and app (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, No app); an optional background video or picture.
- Output: a phone preview, one card per screen with seconds to read (2.8 words a second, at least 1.5 s), a one-line verdict under the phone, and a "Why" list.
- Buttons: Save this caption as an image, Save all of them (PNG), Copy the text.
- State: none kept. Redraws 120 ms after typing.

### Recipe scaler (scaler.html)
- Promise: "Paste a recipe and say who's eating. You get amounts you can actually measure." Search description adds "plus when to split into batches."
- Inputs: ingredients textarea; Recipe serves (min 1, default 4); Grown-ups (min 0, default 2); Kids (min 0, default 20, counted as half a portion).
- Output: factor (×N), a table of amount, ingredient and note (rounded up, weights, sticks of butter), a batch warning over ×3, and a choice of "The whole amount" or "One batch".
- Buttons: Copy the shopping list, Print it.
- State: none kept.

### Packed-decimal decoder (comp3.html)
- Promise: "Paste the bytes of a COMP-3 field and its picture clause. See every digit, the sign and the value…"
- Inputs: bytes in hex (default `03 50 00 0C`), picture clause (default `S9(2)V9(5)`, a full COBOL line also works). Mode: Bytes to value, Value to bytes. Chips: Exhibit A as 3.5, Exhibit A as 0.035, A negative balance, A damaged field.
- Output: a verdict, value, digits before and after the point, sign, field length, every half-byte, and for rate-shaped fields the interest each reading charges on $1,000.
- State: the URL hash holds `m`, `v` and `p`, written with replaceState and read on load.

### Website checkup (checkup.html and _build/worker/checkup.js)
- Promise: "Paste your website's address. You get a plain list of what's broken, worst first, and whether AI tools can find your business."
- Input: one text box. Button: Check it. The page posts to the dj-checkup Worker and gives up after 120 s.
- Worker: refuses private names and addresses (by name, then by a DNS lookup over HTTPS), follows up to 3 redirects, 8 s per request, reads up to 1.5 MB, tests up to 25 action links within a budget of 46 requests, reads robots.txt, meta tags, structured data and page text.
- Output: "N things to fix" worst first, what works, the action-link note, an AI card, and a facts table (Name, Phone, Address, Hours).
- State: none kept.

## What jsdom can't show

- Layout, overlap and tap-target size at 320 and 390 px; real fonts (the caption fitter measured in node-canvas's fallback font, not Barlow, so widths in H-41, H-44 and H-45 are approximate).
- Real speed: H-34 hangs in any engine (an endless loop), but H-35's seconds are jsdom's; the measurement count is exact.

- The live Workers, real DNS, real redirects and Cloudflare's own network limits.
- Keyboard and screen-reader behaviour beyond focus (H-53).

## Differed from this plan

- jsdom is pinned to 26.1.0, because jsdom 31 has no ResourceLoader to keep every outside resource off the network. The `canvas` package was added so the caption fitter could measure text.
- The seven fresh hunters wrote their repros in a scratch folder. The kept ones were copied here as `H-<nn>-<tool>.mjs`, with the harness path made relative, plus their two shared helpers (`lib-checkup.mjs`, `lib-scaler.mjs`) and `run_all.mjs`. Three repros are my own (H-23, H-33, H-47). My other finds duplicated a hunter's, and the hunter's repro is the one kept.
- One hunter's test page used a person's name as a café name; it was changed to "Café Verde" before commit.
- The checkup hunter ran one read-only `git status` against its brief; it changed nothing.
- The 120-second page timeout (H-37) is shown by speeding the Worker's timers 1,000 times, not by waiting.
- Work happened on review/H-01, the branch the prompt names.
- Repros were run in America/Los_Angeles; the gate proof also in UTC.
- Not filed: "Thanks again for dinner. See you Monday. Drive safe." gets the three-short-sentences mark, which is the rule as written; a "Menu" toggle button counts as an action link; pasting 20,001 bytes into the decoder took 3.3 s in jsdom.
