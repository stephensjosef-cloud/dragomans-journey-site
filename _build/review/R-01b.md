# R-01b: a second, independent pre-marketing review of dragomansjourney.com

Read-only review of the whole site the way strangers will meet it. Nothing on the live site, in the build, in the tests, in QUEUE.md or on main was changed; the only files added are in `_build/review/` on the branch `review/R-01`.

- Date: 2026-11-23. Base: origin/main `88f6094` (the T-45 home line is live: "Hey, I'm Josef, your dragoman." is on the home page).
- What was reviewed: the repo root served locally (`python -m http.server`), 19 `.html` pages (18 public pages plus `404.html`). Seven pages (home, Local, Mainframe, Start, Call, Work, Studio) were also fetched from https://dragomansjourney.com and are byte-identical to the repo (line endings aside), so the local copy is the live site. The og images on the live domain are the same sizes as the repo's.
- How: scripted walks in Chromium (Playwright) at 320, 390 and 1440 wide with web fonts loaded and blocked (114 full-page screenshots, kept outside the repo); mechanical angles by script; seven fresh reviewers, each a new subagent with a new browser context, empty storage and only its quoted brief; every reviewer claim was checked by the lead against the page before it counted. Where the lead reproduced a claim it says so; anything not reproduced is marked UNCONFIRMED.
- Safety: no form was submitted to a live Worker and nothing was sent. Every `*.workers.dev` request was stubbed.

## What would lose a customer

22 findings: **5 LOSES CUSTOMERS, 8 HURTS TRUST, 9 POLISH.** Ranked most likely to lose a customer first. Screenshot paths are under `_build/review/shots/`.

**The five most likely to lose a customer, one line each:**
1. A stranger can't check any of it: one unnamed client, no quote, no reference, no reel to watch, and the home page shows no proof at all.
2. On a phone, the home page's first screen never says what he sells or who it's for, and the only contact action ("Free 15-min call") is hidden behind Menu.
3. Pricing is "from" almost everywhere, with a yearly fee that has no number and a three-month minimum, next to the words "fixed price".
4. The Mainframe page's core claim (old and new run on the same data and compared) is admitted "hasn't been done yet" in small grey text, under a hero that says it as fact, from a seller with no bank credential or reference.
5. The Busywork receipt's Copy, Print and "Bring this receipt to a Blueprint" buttons cannot be tapped on a phone at any scroll position.

Evidence: local.html at 390, the results receipt and FAQ; `local-390-header-no-menu.jpg`, `home-390-first-screen.jpg`, `local-390-fit-lists-and-photo.jpg`. **Smallest fix:** one named quote or reference from the nonprofit (with permission) on Local and the case study, and one 20-second watchable reel on the case-study page.
